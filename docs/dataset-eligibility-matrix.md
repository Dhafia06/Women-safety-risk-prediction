## Cross-dataset eligibility notes

- **Primary analysis:** none of the five files is cleared as a primary predictive-analysis dataset. The observed files lack consistent exact provenance/rights or geography/category definitions; the synthetic file is not empirical evidence.
- **Supplementary descriptive candidates:** the IPC 2001–2012 OGD/NCRB candidate and 2013 OGD/NCRB state/UT resource have official-source support and may be used narrowly after verifying the record-level license metadata, preserving attribution, and applying their aggregate/reporting-unit constraints.
- **Quarantine:** the two secondary combined datasets remain out of analysis until exact source versions and rights are established. They may not be silently promoted because their schemas or values resemble official tables.
- **Testing:** only the synthetic file is eligible as a software fixture, with a visible synthetic label and no observed-data joins.
- **No route inference:** all observed files are annual aggregates at state/UT or district/reporting-unit level. None supports street-, route-, or time-of-day safety claims.

## Unresolved items before preprocessing

1. Save source-resource identifiers, retrieval date, exact source artifacts, local SHA-256 values, and record-level license metadata in a provenance manifest. Hashing local files is allowed; replacing them is not.
2. Obtain the NCRB source workbook/table notes for 2001–2012 and determine why two different `JAMMU & KASHMIR / RAILWAYS / 2010` rows appear. Until then preserve both and quarantine that key from unique-geography analysis.
3. Resolve official annual crime-head definitions and year changes; do not sum aggregate, parent, and subcategory fields.
4. Build a dated state/district and reporting-unit crosswalk from official historical records. Current names/codes are not sufficient for historical boundary identity.
5. Identify precise source/license metadata for the 2001–2014 and 2001–2021 combined copies; otherwise continue to exclude them from analysis.
